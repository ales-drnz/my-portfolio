Three ways to centre a triangle in a circle

A · Box centred

The bounding box sits on the centre. Only the bottom corners reach the circle, so the mark looks a little low.

B · Strictly inscribed

The smallest circle around the mark is the guide itself. Top and bottom corners all touch it, but the mark rides high with empty space below.

C · Optical (applied)

The weight of the cells sits on the centre and the outer corners touch the guide circle. Balanced to the eye; this is what the logo now uses.
